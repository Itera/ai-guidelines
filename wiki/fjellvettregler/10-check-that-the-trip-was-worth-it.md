# 10. Check that the trip was worth it

How to measure whether AI actually improves delivery.

Rule: [AI-Fjellvettregler, rule 10](../../guidelines/fjellvettregler.md#10-check-that-the-trip-was-worth-it)

## How to follow it

- Measure before and after you change how you use AI, so you have a baseline.
- Use delivery metrics like the [DORA metrics](https://dora.dev/guides/dora-metrics-four-keys/): deployment frequency, lead time for changes, change failure rate and time to restore service.
- Look at quality too: bugs, rework and review time, not just speed.
- Keep what works and drop what doesn't. Share the results with the team.
