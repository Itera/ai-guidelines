# 2. Tell someone where you're going

How to agree on AI use with the customer before the work starts.

Rule: [AI-Fjellvettregler, rule 2](../../guidelines/fjellvettregler.md#2-tell-someone-where-youre-going)

## How to follow it

Settle these with the customer, preferably in writing, before you start:

- **Tools:** which AI tools and models the team will use.
- **Data:** what code, documents and data the AI may see, and what is off limits.
- **Hosting:** whether data may leave the customer's environment or a given region.
- **Cost:** whether AI costs are billed to the customer or included in Itera's delivery.
- **Changes:** how you'll agree on new tools or wider data access later.

Revisit the agreement when the team, tools or scope change.
