# 6. Know your provisions

How to keep track of AI costs and match the model to the task.

Rule: [AI-Fjellvettregler, rule 6](../../guidelines/fjellvettregler.md#6-know-your-provisions)

## How to follow it

- Know the price of the models you use and check your usage regularly.
- Set a budget with alerts, so you don't run out during something critical like a production release.
- Use a cheaper, faster model or lower effort level for simple tasks. Save the most capable model for hard ones.
- Keep context lean: short instruction files, focused prompts, and new sessions for new tasks.
- Compare models for coding tasks with [DeepSWE](https://deepswe.datacurve.ai/).
