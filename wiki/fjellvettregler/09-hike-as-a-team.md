# 9. Hike as a team

How to share AI setups, skills and lessons so the whole team benefits.

Rule: [AI-Fjellvettregler, rule 9](../../guidelines/fjellvettregler.md#9-hike-as-a-team)

## How to follow it

- Commit shared setup to the repo (instruction files, permission rules, skills) so everyone who clones it gets the same.
- Build AI into team processes like issue planning and PR review, not just individual work.
- Share lessons learned in this wiki. See [Contributing](../../README.md#contributing).
- Share reusable skills in [`skills/`](../../skills/).

## Recommended skills and tools

- [`dev-foundation-setup`](../../skills/dev-foundation-setup/SKILL.md) gives a team a shared, committed AI setup. See [Project setup](../project-setup.md).
