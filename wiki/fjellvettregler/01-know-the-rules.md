# 1. Know the rules before you head out

How to get familiar with the policies and regulations that apply before you start using AI on a task.

Rule: [AI-Fjellvettregler, rule 1](../../guidelines/fjellvettregler.md#1-know-the-rules-before-you-head-out)

## How to follow it

- Read Itera's AI Use Policy (linked from the [guideline](../../guidelines/fjellvettregler.md#1-know-the-rules-before-you-head-out)) before your first AI-assisted task, and again when it changes.
- Check whether the customer has its own AI policy. If it does, it applies on top of Itera's.
- Know the main regulations that may apply, such as GDPR and the EU AI Act, and whether the task touches regulated data or high-risk use.
- Unsure? Ask before you start, not after.

## Recommended skills and tools

- [`fjellvettregler-check`](../../skills/fjellvettregler-check/SKILL.md) asks whether you know the policy and the regulations that apply.
